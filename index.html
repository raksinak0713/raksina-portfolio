About

Design is my ever-curious seat.

I am a Bangkok-based graphic designer with a background in Interior Design (Assumption University of Thailand) and advanced studies in Graphic Design at Otis College of Art and Design, USA. I thrive on discovering something new at every project.

My work spans graphic design, brand identity, UX/UI, storyboarding, illustration, and photography. I work fluidly across Thai, English, and Japanese — bridging cultures through visual communication.
